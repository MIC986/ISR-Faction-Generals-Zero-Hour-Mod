## Install

You need Windows, a legitimate copy of *Zero Hour* updated to version 1.04, [GenLauncher](https://www.moddb.com/mods/genlauncher) (Optional), the Microsoft Visual C++ 2015–2022 Redistributable (x86), and .NET Framework 4.5 or later.

1. Download [**ISR-Faction-2.0.0-Portable.zip**](https://github.com/MIC986/ISR-Faction-Generals-Zero-Hour-Mod/releases) from the GitHub Release.
2. Put the **!!ISRFaction-2.0.0.big** file into the game folder or in GenLauncher as a mod.
3. Run the EXE **once** and **only** if you have any of these in your game: Custom control bar or hotkey pack (etc... Control Bar Pro) or Non-English game. If not, there is no needed to touch that EXE. Then if you want to intsall on GenLauncher remove `!!ISRFaction-2.0.0.big` from the game folder and install it in GenLauncher as mod instead.
4. Now you can play bt launching Zero Hour if installed on game folder or launching the mod from GenLauncher. 

For removal, if installed on the game folder remove `!!ISRFaction-2.0.0.big` or if on GenLauncher click on the version then delete.

## About the mod

**ISR Faction** adds four Israeli armies to *Command & Conquer: Generals Zero Hour*, combining Israeli military equipment with Zero Hour’s base building, promotions and battlefield tactics.

Deploy **Merkava tanks, Namer heavy APCs, Spike missile teams, M109 artillery and F-35I Adir fighters**. Build a C4I network, develop specialist technology, and choose between combined-arms assaults, precision air operations, layered defenses or intelligence-led attacks.

### Choose your army

| Army | Main strength | Main weakness |
|---|---|---|
| **Israel** | Flexible combined arms, durable armor and battlefield support | Expensive equipment and exposed supply lines |
| **Israel Precision Air** | Veteran aircraft, stealth strikes and aerial protection | Costly aircraft losses and fewer ground options |
| **Israel Air Defense** | Missile interception, mobile air defenses and strategic protection | Saturation attacks, artillery and direct ground assaults |
| **Israel Intelligence** | Reconnaissance, sabotage, target designation and deception | Detectors, fragile specialists and limited heavy air support |

<details>
<summary><b>Israel - Combined-arms warfare</b></summary>

Build a balanced force around **Merkava Mk. III and Mk. IV Trophy tanks**, **Namer heavy APCs**, **Eitan wheeled carriers** and **M109 Doher howitzers**. Support them with infantry, aircraft, reconnaissance and medical vehicles.

- **Eitan APC:** A fast wheeled transport carrying eight infantry.
- **Field Workshop:** Produces field engineers and provides **Field Repair Surge** to help sustain ground offensives.
- **Reserve Platoon:** Delivers rifle infantry and Spike teams.
- **Heavy Reserve Column:** Mobilizes armored reinforcements beside headquarters; they must drive to the front.
- **Coordinated Artillery:** Bombards a selected area with conventional artillery rounds.

**Best for:** Players who want to combine armor, infantry, artillery and aircraft rather than depend on one specialist weapon.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Israel Precision Air - Control the skies, strike key targets</b></summary>

Build an air force with **F-16I Sufa interceptors and strike aircraft**, **F-15I Raam bombers**, **F-35I Adir stealth fighters** and **AH-64D Saraf helicopters**.

- **F-35I Air Superiority:** A dedicated air-combat variant of the Adir.
- **F-16I Electronic Escort:** An escort fighter supporting aircraft formations.
- **Hermes 450 ISR UAV:** An unarmed reconnaissance aircraft that detects concealed enemies.
- **Airborne Laser Defense:** Research missile-defense lasers for aircraft, helicopters and UAVs after reaching **General rank 3** and building a **C4I Network Center**.
- **Strike Coordination:** Temporarily improves nearby aircraft damage and fire rate.
- **Suppression Window:** Briefly disables enemy ground vehicles, factories and defenses to create an attack opportunity.
- **Adir Interdiction and Bunker Breaker:** Additional precision-strike options.
- **Flight Support Center:** Provides local aircraft recovery.

**Best for:** Coordinated attacks on valuable targets. Aircraft still need ammunition, protected airfields and careful routes through enemy defenses.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Israel Air Defense - Build a layered defensive network</b></summary>

Combine **Iron Dome, Iron Beam, mobile SAM systems and radar support** to protect your army and infrastructure.

- **Iron Dome:** Launches Tamir interceptors against eligible incoming missiles. Its launcher-missile upgrade extends protection against **Tomahawk and SCUD Launcher missiles**.
- **Iron Beam:** Uses a laser to destroy eligible incoming missiles. Separate upgrades add **aircraft/helicopter targeting** and **large launcher-missile interception**.
- **Beam Optics:** C4I research increases Iron Beam range by **20%**.
- **Compact Laser Defense:** A **$500 upgrade purchased separately for each eligible vehicle**. Requires C4I and provides short-range, chance-based interception of small missiles.
- **Stunner Battery and ELM-2084 Radar:** Specialist mobile air-defense and reconnaissance options.
- **Mobile Fire Control:** Temporarily improves mobile anti-aircraft weapon range and fire rate.
- **Interceptor Service Depot:** Provides a temporary boost to nearby defensive fire.

**Arrow 3** replaces the offensive strategic superweapon with defensive interception, including a **five-minute strategic charge** against Chinese nuclear missiles and SCUD Storm missiles.

**Best for:** Protecting important positions and advancing behind defensive coverage. Interceptors can be overwhelmed, and missile defense does not stop conventional tank shells.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Israel Intelligence - Find weaknesses and exploit them</b></summary>

Use concealed scouts, forward observers and electronic warfare to prepare attacks before committing your main force.

- **Field Observation Team:** An unarmed concealed observer that scouts, detects stealth and designates enemy targets.
- **Maglan Precision ATGM Team:** Gains extra effectiveness against designated targets.
- **Harop Loitering-Munition Launcher:** Attacks from long range with interceptable attack drones.
- **Signals Warfare Vehicle:** Approaches enemy infrastructure to disrupt its operation.
- **Covert Reconnaissance Team:** Scouts and performs close-range building sabotage.
- **Specialist Sayeret Commando:** Has expanded reconnaissance, designation, disruption and sabotage abilities.
- **Electronic Blackout:** Temporarily disables vehicles and powered defenses.
- **Coordinated Targeting Network:** Marks enemies to strengthen coordinated attacks.
- **Signal Ambush:** Temporarily increases nearby Intelligence infantry weapon damage.
- **Decoy Column:** Deploys harmless remote tank decoys to draw enemy attention and fire.
- **Intercepted Transfers:** A five-star ability that diverts available enemy money from a visible supply center.

**Best for:** Scouting, flanking and carefully timed assaults. Your specialists need protection, and enemy detectors can expose covert operations.

</details>

### Strategic weapons and army development

**Operation Iron Horizon**, available to Israel, Precision Air and Intelligence, sends **three protected strategic aircraft carrying six heavy bombs**. It has a **nine-minute charge** and requires a powered Strategic Strike Command.

Develop your army through **C4I research**, General’s promotions and faction-specific support buildings. **Technology Export Offices** provide late-game income through investment in specialist export contracts.

**Skirmish AI and multiplayer are supported.** All multiplayer participants should use the same mod and custom engine version.
